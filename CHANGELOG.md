# Changelog

## v0.6.0 (first public release)
- Brag lines and milestone announcements now start with **[Chronicles of Azeroth]**
  so friends know where they came from.
- New `/coa feedback` (also `/coa ideas`, `/coa bug`): shows a copyable link to
  report a bug or suggest a milestone or challenge.
- Release packaging for CurseForge, Wago and WoWInterface.

## v0.5.1
- Brag to Say now puts the line in your chat box for you to send (the game does
  not let addons speak in Say).
- If the game ever blocks a chat line from the addon, Brag falls back to the chat
  box and announcements pause until you reload.

## v0.5.0
- **Sharing**: optional milestone announcements to guild or party (off by
  default, rate-limited), a Brag button, and **Fellows**: inspect another player
  who uses the addon and compare chronicles side by side.

## v0.4.0
- Every chapter redesigned as a Quest Log style open book.

## v0.3.0
- **Challenges**: accept up to five of 19 challenges, with notifications.
- Blizzard-style UI redesign.

## v0.2.0
- Exploration, progression tracking and 45 milestones with titles.

## v0.1.0
- First version: auto-written journal, character profile and event history.
